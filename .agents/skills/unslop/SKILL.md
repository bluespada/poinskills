# Unslop

## When to activate
- Activate on clean this text, remove slop, or make this sound human.
- Skip fact checking, and skip code style, since this covers prose only.
- Treat sterile polished text as suspect until rhythm and voice land.

## Strip AI tells
- Cut superficial -ing tails like highlighting or ensuring, plus vague experts believe lines, by naming sources or deleting.
- Swap AI vocabulary like delve, crucial, pivotal, landscape, tapestry, underscore, vibrant, plus serves as or boasts, for plain is or has or use.
- Cut not just X but Y framing, forced groups of three, synonym cycling, and false from X to Y ranges, using the natural count.
- Ban em dashes entirely and mid-sentence colons, using periods or commas, and fix title case headings, curly quotes, emoji, boldface abuse, and label colon lists.
- Delete chatbot phrases like hope this helps and sycophancy like great question, plus filler like in order to, stacked hedges, and generic futures look bright endings.
- Swap abstract nouns like substrate, vector, nexus, paradigm, flywheel, north star for their concrete word like base, way, or limit.
- Say mechanism or number not feeling, split dense sentences to one idea each, prefer active voice with a named actor, and cut adverbs propping weak verbs.

## Add human unevenness
- Repair rhythm by varying sentence length, breaking one smooth paragraph with a short one, since even length reads machine made.
- Add texture with one concrete aside, named doubt, or irregular sentence per page, since near-zero error polish is itself a tell.
- Rotate syntax so no three sentences open alike, mixing simple, compound, and short punch shapes with varied noun verb pairings.
- Break fingerprint runs like repeated adjective noun chains or identical part of speech runs that simple classifiers catch.
- Treat the blocklist as sampled not fixed, scanning for whichever word repeats thrice where a human would vary it, since tells drift over time.
- Restore voice with one opinion plus reason, one concrete number, and one named actor per page, then cut any sentence that fits another project unchanged.

## Process and self-check
- Scan for tells above, rewrite preserving meaning and tone, then reread asking what still smells machine made.
- Run as its own pass after drafting, keep edits as proposals, and never drop facts to sound plainer.
